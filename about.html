The ACCREU Data Catalog provides a central entry point for discovering and accessing datasets produced within the ACCREU project (Assessing Climate Change Risk in EUrope). Explore quantitative data on the economic, physical and biophysical impacts of climate change and adaptation, covering agriculture and land use, water, ecosystems and biodiversity, energy, labour, coastal impacts, wildfire, and the economy, across a wide range of spatial scales and modelling approaches.

Throughout the project, IIASA has maintained ACCREU's internal data repository through the IIASA Accelerator, supporting the management, sharing and use of data among the 15 ACCREU partners. This catalog builds on that infrastructure to provide a single public entry point for the datasets released by ACCREU partners, and brings together datasets published through established research repositories such as Zenodo. Each dataset links to the ACCREU deliverable that describes it.

How to use the catalog

  • Zenodo ↗ opens the permanent, citable record. The file list shown in the catalog is the content of that record, including the files inside each zip archive.
  • Request access marks data held on the IIASA Accelerator that is not public yet: review the data use terms and email the dataset owner.
  • D2.4 ↗ and similar badges open the ACCREU deliverable report for the dataset.
  • Ask AI finds the datasets, Zenodo records and deliverable report passages that answer your question, and explains them in plain language with numbered links to each source (reports open at the cited page). It only answers from ACCREU material.
